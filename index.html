Narendhiran Vijayakumar

I recently completed my B.Tech. in Mechanical Engineering with a minor in Computer Science at NIT Tiruchirappalli. My research interests lie broadly in robotics and embodied AI, with a focus on robot learning, long-horizon manipulation, task and motion planning, and visual perception.

I enjoy building systems that connect perception and reasoning to real robot behavior, especially in settings where robots must adapt to changing scenes, incomplete observations, and long-horizon tasks.
