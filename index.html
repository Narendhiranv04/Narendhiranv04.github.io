Narendhiran Vijayakumar

I'm a research assistant at IIIT Hyderabad. I completed my B.Tech. in Mechanical Engineering with a minor in Computer Science at NIT Tiruchirappalli.

My research interests lie in robotics and embodied AI, particularly long-horizon manipulation, task and motion planning, robot learning, and visual perception. I am interested in systems that connect perception and reasoning to reliable robot behavior under changing scenes and partial observability.
