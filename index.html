Task & Motion Planning · Robot Learning · Embodied Perception

Narendhiran Vijayakumar

I am a research assistant at IIIT Hyderabad, working on robotics and embodied AI. I completed my B.Tech. in Mechanical Engineering with a minor in Computer Science at NIT Tiruchirappalli.

My research focuses on long-horizon manipulation, task and motion planning, robot learning, and visual perception. I am interested in systems that reason under partial observability, adapt when new constraints are discovered, and connect learned representations with reliable robot execution.

Current direction foundation-model-centric TAMP / learning from demonstration / latent representations of infeasibility / selective replanning / contact-rich manipulation
